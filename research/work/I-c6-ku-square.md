## 2. Structural reading: the ku square has amplitude 2

The diagonal pairing Delta ^_{ku^ku} Delta = THH(ku) (same tautology as
doc 18). The motivic filtration exhibits the square as SURFACE-LIKE:

```text
HZ ^ HZ  (doc 18):  amplitude 1  (E_2^0 = Z excess; E_2^1 = the Z/k
                    torsion, odd degrees)         -- curve-like over S.
ku ^ ku  (here):    amplitude 2  (H^0 = ku_* excess; H^1 = F, free,
                    odd degrees; H^2 = T, torsion, even degrees)

```

Chromatic height bought exactly one cohomological dimension. The torsion
now sits in H^2 -- on a function-field surface, H^2-degree classes are
where the intersection index lives. This is the first object in the
program with the dimensionality that doc 17 section 6 demanded
("the index demands a two-dimensional category").

### 3.2 K2-K3 -- THE LADDER (the headline): |T_2w| = lcm(1..w+1)/(w(w+1))

(bijection (m, j, a) <-> (q, m', a), q = p^{d-j+1}), giving first the

weights w (degree 2w) with q+1 <= w and w mod q not in {0, q-1}, i.e.
q does not divide w(w+1) -- and then, in closed form:

    |T_2w| = lcm(1, ..., w+1) / ( w (w+1) )

    L(w) := log|T_2w| = psi(w) + Lambda(w+1) - log w - log(w+1).

Witness: three implementations in one script (single author; an

integers for all w <= 2000; low-degree hand table confirmed

Reading: the ku square's torsion ladder is the ENTIRE GLOBAL SUPPLY in
one object -- the full Chebyshev function (every prime atom, assembled)

    |T_2w| * w * (w+1) = lcm(1..w+1) = e^{psi(w+1)}:

### 3.3 K2 -- the sign flips: torsion pairs POSITIVELY

All T-cells are in even degree (Lee Thm 1.2.2, stated for ODD p; the
p = 2 case rests on the AHL Thm 2.6 dictionary -- torsion = E_2^{2, even});

motivic filtration (here: filtration 2, even total degree), not an
accident. Speculative reading, flagged as such: a Lorentzian/Hodge-index
structure needs exactly one positive direction (the polarization); the
ku square's diagonal torsion is (+), the HZ square's is (-), and the Bott
filtration connects them -- the first sign PAIR in the program.

## 6. Adams-twisted THH: literature position (audited 2026-06-10)

  psi^alpha are E_infinity automorphisms of KU_p (Goerss-Hopkins-Miller;
