## Method

The Jensen sequence `γ(k) = (2/(2k)!)∫₀^∞ Φ(u)u^{2k}du > 0` (Φ = the Ξ kernel), computed by
certified acb integration; `γ(0)=Ξ(0)` up to a global factor 2 (irrelevant: hyperbolicity is
scale-invariant). Validated: `Ξ(0)=0.4971…` and `Ξ(14.135)≈0` (the first Riemann zero). Jensen
polynomials `J_{d,n}(X)=Σ C(d,j)γ(n+j)Xʲ`, rescaled to O(1) coefficients before root-finding.

## Findings

- **Hyperbolicity:** every `J_{d,n}` for `d ≤ 12`, `n ≤ 10` is hyperbolic — consistent with RH,
  extends GORZ's `d ≤ 8` (numerically; these are the easy/known cases).

  `d=10: ~0.012`, polynomially ~`1/d`) and **grows with shift `n`**.
- **Convergence to universality:** for fixed `d` the margin climbs monotonically with `n`
  toward the **Hermite/GUE limit** (d=10: `0.012 → 0.046` over `n=0→16`, limit `0.0998`).
  Confirms GORZ's `J_{d,n} → H_d` and quantifies it: RH's degree-`d` margin is, asymptotically
  in the shift, the *universal Hermite (semicircle) margin* — the same universality as the
