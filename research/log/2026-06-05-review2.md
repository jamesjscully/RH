## 1. Structural flaw — fixed-τ has no intermediate rung

The Wei–Wu/Ge criterion is **fixed-τ**: bounded norm at τ ⟺ ζ zero-free in `Re s>τ`.
Decisive fact:

> No fixed half-plane `Re s>τ₀` with `τ₀<1` is known to be zero-free. Every
> unconditional zero-free result in 120 years — de la Vallée Poussin (1899),
> Vinogradov–Korobov (1958) — is a *height-dependent region* `σ>1−c/(log t)^θ` that
> narrows to the line as `t→∞`. None gives a fixed `τ₀<1`.

So the fixed-τ form is all-or-nothing: `Re s>1` is free, the next fixed-τ result is
already RH-strength. F3's "push to some fixed `τ₀∈(½,1)`" is not a sub-target — it is
the whole mountain. **Fix (F5):** reformulate the forward target as height-localized
(`T`-windowed bounded norm ↔ region `σ>1−c/log T`), so the machinery can engage the
real frontier. Go/no-go: can it reproduce de la Vallée Poussin? If not, it is weaker
than 1899. If yes, the target is *improving* a zero-free region — a publishable sub-RH
result the fixed-τ form cannot offer.

## 3. Harness gap — "fails for DH" is necessary but not sufficient (H4) — IMPLEMENTED

DH has no Euler product, so the DH wall is a one-sided filter. But having an Euler
product does **not** imply RH: **Epstein zeta** of class-number-`>1` quadratic forms has
a functional equation and Dirichlet series yet provably carries zeros off the line and
in `σ>1` (Davenport–Heilbronn 1936; Potter–Titchmarsh 1935). A lemma can fail for DH and
still be on a false path. Add a **second wall**: a critical-path lemma must fail for
*both* DH (no product) and the second wall (product structure, wrong RH status), pinning
the load-bearing property to the full Selberg-class package (primitive, degree 1,
Ramanujan), not bare product existence.

**Implemented this session.** `EpsteinZeta` (`witness/objects.py`) evaluates the Epstein
zeta of the non-principal disc-`−23` form `Q1=(2,1,3)` (class number 3) by the
Chowla–Selberg / Terras incomplete-gamma expansion:

```
Λ_Q(s) = Σ'_v (πQ(v))^{-s}Γ(s,πQ(v)) + (1/√Δ)Σ'_v (πQ*(v))^{s-1}Γ(1-s,πQ*(v))
         + (1/√Δ)/(s-1) − 1/s,        Z_Q(s) = π^s Λ_Q(s)/Γ(s),
```

with `A=[[a,b/2],[b/2,c]]`, `Δ=det A`, `Q*(v)=vᵀA⁻¹v`. Validated: exact match to
`Z_{m²+n²}=4ζ(s)β(s)`, and agreement with the naive lattice sum for the disc-`−23` form.
(Bug found & fixed in the derivation: an early `(πQ)^{+s}` sign error — `acb.neg()` is not
negation; use explicit `(-s)`.)

The wall is certified **structurally** by `witness/second_wall.py` (harness Check 1b), no
zero-hunt required:

1. **No single Euler product** — `r_Q1(1)=0` (a product needs `f(1)=1`), and
   `r(2)·r(3)=4 ≠ r(6)=2` (non-multiplicative).
2. **Genus sum = Dedekind = `ζ·L`** — `(1/2)(r₀+2r₁)(n)=Σ_{d|n}(−23/d)` for all `n≤40`
   (ideal counts), and analytically `(1/2)(Z_{Q0}+2Z_{Q1})(s)=ζ(s)L(s,χ₋₂₃)` (matches to
   `1e-7…1e-11` at `s=2,2.5,3`). So `Z_Q1` is a genuine combination of **product-bearing
   constituents**.
3. **RH-false** — Davenport–Heilbronn 1936: zeros in `Re s>1`.

So product structure is present yet `Z_Q1` violates RH: a lemma whose lever is "has an
Euler product" passes the *constituents* but must still reject `Z_Q1` — only the finer
Selberg-class structure can.

**Not pinned:** a concrete off-line zero. The disc-`−23` `σ>1` zeros are sparse and at
large height — a `|Z_Q|` scan over `Re s∈[0.55,1.4]`, `|Im s|≤80` finds none (floor
`~0.14`), consistent with the literature. Cataloguing one via a high-height
argument-principle search is a follow-up; RH-falsity itself is a theorem, so the wall
stands without it.

## 4. The ignored frontier — Li/Weil positivity, de Bruijn–Newman (E4)

The reframe proved the Hilbert-distance reformulation is RH-neutral, yet keeps it as the
primary engine while using the explicit formula only as a discriminator (Check 4). But
the explicit formula is exactly where unconditional RH-flavored theorems get *proven*:
Li coefficients (`λ_n≥0 ⟺ RH`, concrete and computable) and the de Bruijn–Newman
constant (`RH ⟺ Λ≤0`, with `Λ≥0` proved unconditionally by Rodgers–Tao 2020 via
zero-flow dynamics — a genuine two-sided unconditional handle). E4: spike a finite
Li/Weil functional and test whether the witness coordinates add anything the bare
explicit formula does not; if not, pivot primary effort there.
