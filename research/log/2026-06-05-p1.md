## Certified (P1a)

- **`disc(H_n) = 2^{3n(n−1)/2} · ∏_{k=1}^{n} kᵏ`**, exact — verified `n = 2..12` by exact
  integer equality (FLINT `fmpz_poly.resultant`; `disc = (−1)^{n(n−1)/2} Res(H_n,H_n')/lc`).
- Hence **`log|disc(H_n)| = (3/2)n(n−1)ln2 + Σₖ k ln k`** exactly, so
  `log|disc(H_n)|/n² → ½ln n + ((3/2)ln2 − ¼)` (n=20: 2.312 vs predicted 2.288, converging).

Two pieces, both meaningful:
- `½ln n` = the **semicircle / GUE log-energy** — the *same* universality as the zeros' pair
  correlation (Montgomery). The proof's slack and the zeros' statistics share one energy.
- `(3/2)ln2 = 1.0397` = the **exact 2-power structural constant** — this is the recognizable
  constant the Bombieri–Weyl slack rate (~1.04, log 2026-06-05-jensen-real) reflects. So the
  slack's leading constant is rigorously a discriminant-structural (height) constant, not a
  numerical accident.

## Honest scope

The discriminant — the *energy/height* — is pinned exactly. The slack *distance* to the
discriminant variety carries, in addition, the **gradient/conditioning factor** of the variety;
the BW-normalized discriminant alone does not reproduce the monotone slack decay (its
per-degree rate starts near `(3/2)ln2` at small `n` but diverges as the BW-norm term takes
over). So the exact slack constant is *consistent with* `(3/2)ln2` but not yet fully certified.
