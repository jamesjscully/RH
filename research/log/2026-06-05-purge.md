# Reorientation + purge — 2026-06-05

Recorded the strategic lessons of this cycle into `STRATEGY.md` (the single source of
truth) and **deleted the futility apparatus** so the project cannot drift back into it.

## Why

The Nyman–Beurling / witness / forward-convergence program was an **information-dispersing
reformulation** with **no reverse force** (STRATEGY §0–§2): `forward = RH`, reverse trivial,
geometry RH-neutral (holds for Davenport–Heilbronn). The descent micro-test confirmed it
structurally — the explicit-formula family is additive → contractible nerve → `H¹ = 0` →
descent-trivial. Keeping its tooling around is the main risk of relapse.

## Jensen-cover nerve (set up this cycle)

`jensen_nerve.py` computes the descent structure of cert/tail/bridge proofs:
- a pure recursion is a **tree** → `H¹ = 0` → descent-trivial (same fate as NB-BD);
- teeth need **shared sub-problems** (reconvergent cycles, verified: a 2-diamond DAG gives
  `H¹ = 2`; a holed `(d,n)` cover gives `H¹ = 1`) **and** nontrivial **slack monodromy**
  (product of overlap margin-ratios `≠ 1`, demoed).
Next: plug in a real Jensen (or interval-subdivision) proof's overlap graph + margin ratios.
If `H¹ = 0` and monodromy `= 1` everywhere, descent is dead for RH-shaped proofs (a real
negative result); otherwise that family is where an obstruction calculus could live.
