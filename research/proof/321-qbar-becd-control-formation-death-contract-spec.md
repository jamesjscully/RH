## 1. Purpose

The live target now has a final timing loophole:

```text
let a control instantiate the same formal package,
then reject it later by a sign, projection, DD.4, or sample test.
```

That is too late. The square has not killed controls if controls first form
the carrier-shaped object. Formation-level death means the rule that produces
the object, endpoint integrality, degree maps, DD.4 boundary, boundary
pushforward compatibility, graph/composition, Rosati, finite-field counts,
and graph degrees is joint enough that controls cannot instantiate it at all.
