## 1. Purpose

The support-flag repair can now say:

```text
do not merely choose a flag;
use the chain -1,0,1,...,N as a category;
then its incidence/path algebra supplies arrows and composition.
```

This does add composition. But it adds the composition of support
intervals. The arrows are generated by the ordered finite support, not by a
carrier multiplication or correspondence. The path ideal is nilpotent on
truncated support, endpoint lines are still support-vertex choices, and the
bottom corner still recovers the external tensor line rather than inverting
the DMP bottom class.

This document is local. It does not reject a carrier-native correspondence
algebra whose arrows construct the support data. It rejects only the
incidence/path algebra of the visible support chain when no primitive
carrier map constructs those arrows.

## 2. Lemma

```text
Lemma 155.1 (support path algebras are support bookkeeping).
Let M_N = R_DMP[-1..N] tensor L ~= L^r, with r=N+2 and
L=F_2[u^{+-1}]. Let C_r be the total ordered support chain.

The incidence algebra, equivalently the path algebra of the line quiver
with interval paths collapsed to their endpoints, has L-rank

  r(r+1)/2.

For r=3,4,6,10 this gives:

  6,10,21,55.

Its strict non-identity paths are the strict support intervals:

  r(r-1)/2 = 3,6,15,45.

The adjacent arrows are exactly the support-chain relations:

  r-1 = 2,3,5,9.

The strict path ideal is nilpotent: paths of length r vanish on the finite
chain. Hence this algebra does not supply an invertible Laurent shift.

An endpoint/corner line still requires choosing a support vertex. The bottom
endpoint corner is the external scalar line L from the tensor repair; the
original DMP bottom class remains square-zero because there is no degree -2
target. Path composition is interval concatenation, not carrier
multiplication, and it constructs no torsor splitter.

The doc-138 shear obstruction remains res_1(c)=1, the bottom obstruction
remains y^2=0, and the same ordered-chain incidence/path algebra is formable
on controls.

Therefore a support-chain incidence/path algebra is not a construction-grade
R1 carrier correspondence. A surviving route must construct the arrows as
carrier-native correspondences and prove DH/Epstein death at that
construction.
```
