## 1. Purpose

The live target could still be weakened into:

```text
take the prime-power tower in N,
attach endpoint/register data by a codec or valuation character,
then use the carrier language only as a label for the assembled package.
```

That is not a represented Qbar_BECD effectivity domain. It is an external
arithmetic address attached to the carrier. The domain must instead be a
represented internal stratum: it must be generated by the Qbar_BECD carrier
object itself, and every live atom must be typed by that same stratum.

This is the useful remaining distinction after docs 269-271 and 323:
the old no-gos killed rank-one valuation characters, scalar degree codecs,
and free endpoint registers. Doc 323 killed semantic Euler labels and
carrier-blind theta kernels. Doc 324 says what remains admissible: not a
better address label, but an internal Cartier/effectivity stratum tower of
the compactified square.

## 2. Lemma

```text
Lemma 324.1 (the effectivity domain must be internal to Qbar_BECD).

The external prime-power tower is arithmetically real:

    1,2,4,8,16,32,64
    depths 0,1,2,3,4,5,6.

Degree-pair prime-power codecs are also real on finite windows:

    (d1,d2) |-> 2^d1 3^d2

is injective on the tested 4 x 4 window.

Endpoint valuation registers are also real: the same Z^2 register supports
many primitive boundary directions, including

    d1, d2, d1+d2, d1-d2, 2d1+d2.

None of those facts supplies an internal Qbar_BECD domain. The accepted datum
must instead have one represented domain ID:

    D_Qbar_BECD_eff

and the same internal stratum must type:

    effectivity_domain,
    endpoint,
    boundary,
    pushforward,
    graph,
    Rosati,
    count_degree,
    control_death.

Its dependency graph must contain:

    effectivity_domain -- endpoint,
    endpoint -- boundary,
    boundary -- pushforward,
    pushforward -- graph,
    graph -- Rosati,
    graph -- count_degree,
    Rosati -- control_death,
    control_death -- effectivity_domain.

The accepted shape is an internal Cartier/effectivity stratum tower:

    value 1,2,4,8,16,32,
    depth 0,1,2,3,4,5,
    specialization edges 1->2->4->8->16->32,
    endpoint pushforwards typed by the same stratum.

Epstein represented-norm data remains a downstream shadow. It represents
1,4,6,9,36 and has two irreducible factorizations

    36 = 4*9 = 6*6,

so it does not provide the internal tower address. Controls may retain
formal graph/Rosati/count shadows, but they fail at the internal domain.
```

## 3. Proof

`prime_int_n1b_qbar_becd_internal_effectivity_domain_contract` verifies ten
gates.

IED1 requires every live atom to be typed by the same internal stratum.

IED2 rejects the external free prime monoid. Its prime-power tower and
valuation depths are real, but it is not generated by Qbar_BECD object/map/
cycle data.

IED3 rejects prime-power degree-pair codecs. Their finite-window labels are
injective, but endpoint pushforwards and count/degree data are decoder data,
not internal strata.

IED4 rejects endpoint valuation registers. They choose a boundary direction
externally and remain formable on controls.

IED5 rejects products or stacks of local Euler factors. Splitting the domain
across atoms fails the non-split internal stratum requirement.

IED6 records the accepted shape: an internal Cartier/effectivity stratum
tower with specialization maps and endpoint pushforwards typed by the same
domain.

IED7 keeps the Epstein norm shadow downstream. Its theta/count layer remains
alive, but it lacks the internal prime-power tower address.

IED8 rejects controls with formal graph/Rosati/count shadows but no internal
effectivity domain.

IED9 rejects declared internal domain IDs without represented strata.

IED10 records the live predicate. Only the represented internal
effectivity-domain stratum passes.
