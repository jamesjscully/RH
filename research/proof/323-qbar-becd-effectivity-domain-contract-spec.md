## 1. Purpose

The live primitive could still be weakened into:

```text
an Euler/Satake label,
a finite prime sample,
a theta/Poisson kernel that also computes for Epstein,
or a later declaration that controls are excluded.
```

That is not first-generator control death. The first generator must have a
domain on which zeta prime-power towers exist and on which Epstein/DH/control
replacements fail structurally, while their lower shadows may remain alive.

The point is not to append the word "Euler". The point is to represent the
effectivity domain as part of Qbar_BECD's object/map/cycle data and make the
endpoint, boundary, pushforward, graph, Rosati, count/degree, and control
exclusion atoms typed by that domain.
