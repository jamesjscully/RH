## 1. Purpose

After splitter, coinduced, bar-resolution, cone, and jet-deletion repairs
fail, the most direct algebraic move is:

```text
force c to be trivial by quotienting U_1 by the C_2-stable subgroup <c>.
```

This document rejects only that shortcut. The quotient sends `c` to `1` by
definition, but it supplies no carrier boundary. Worse, in the tested
truncations the quotient still has nontrivial `H^1`.

## 2. Lemma

```text
Lemma 262.1 (obstruction quotient is not carrier boundary).

Let

    U_1 = 1 + z F_2[[z]],
    phi(z) = z/(1+z),
    c = (1+z)^-1.

In the base coefficient system, for truncations L=6,8,10,12:

    H^1(<phi>; U_1) = Z/2,
    [c] != 0,
    res_1(c)=1.

Let K=<c> be the cyclic subgroup generated by c. Since

    phi(c) = c^-1,

K is C_2-stable. The quotient

    Q_L = U_{1,L} / <c>

therefore has an induced C_2-action and sends c to the identity. However,
exact finite coset enumeration gives:

    H^1(<phi>; Q_L) = Z/2

for L=6,8,10,12.

Thus quotienting by <c> does not construct the missing boundary map. It
kills the named representative by relation and still leaves a nontrivial
cohomology class in the quotient. The same quotient operation is formable on
arbitrary controls by modding out the span or subgroup generated by a chosen
class.

The obstruction quotient constructs no actual square, integral rep_NS, two
degree maps, indecomposable diagonal, Frobenius graph, proper correspondence
category, fiber-product composition, projection formula, canonical Rosati,
positive primitive sign, carrier boundary, or control death.
```

Verdict label:

```text
N1B_OBSTRUCTION_QUOTIENT__C_EQUALS_ONE_NOT_CARRIER__NO_GO.
```
