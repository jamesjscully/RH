## 1. Purpose

After doc 324, the remaining live dodge is:

```text
first construct endpoint degrees, graph counts, a boundary kernel, Rosati
positivity, or control failure;
then define the "effectivity domain" as the locus where those tests pass.
```

That reverses the construction. It creates a successful diagnostic locus, not
the missing object. The square itself must construct endpoint integrality and
all downstream atoms from the internal effectivity stratum. The effectivity
domain may depend on represented Qbar object/map/cycle evidence, but it may
not depend on endpoint, boundary, pushforward, graph, Rosati, count/degree,
or control-death predicates.

## 3. Proof

`prime_int_n1b_qbar_becd_effectivity_first_generator_orientation_contract`
verifies ten gates.

EFO1 records the accepted directed shape: Qbar object/map/cycle evidence may
feed the internal domain, and the domain must generate the downstream atoms.

EFO2 rejects endpoint-degree fibers. They recover the visible prime-power
tower only by using endpoint data first.

EFO3 rejects graph/count fibers. They are downstream of pushforward and graph
composition and can be retuned.

EFO4 rejects boundary-kernel domains. The DD.4 boundary map cannot be an
input to the first effectivity generator.

EFO5 rejects Rosati-positive and control-failure filters. They occur too
late to explain formation-level control death.

EFO6 rejects post-hoc intersections of all gates.

EFO7 records the control failure mode: controls can mimic downstream
degree/count fibers without representing Qbar_BECD.

EFO8 rejects declared root status without represented strata and generation
edges.

EFO9 makes the endpoint edge mandatory. A first generator that does not
actually generate endpoint integrality is not the needed domain.

EFO10 records the live predicate. Only non-circular effectivity first
generation passes.
