## 2. Lemma

```text
Lemma 194.1 (Naive tolerant smash is only additive log-count data).

Let E_n be the finite interval shadow with |E_n| = 2^n and equip it with
the equality tolerance. For the Q2 candidate relation on E_n x E_m generated
by factor tolerances,

  (x,y) R^wedge (x',y) when x R_1 x',
  (x,y) R^wedge (x,y') when y R_2 y',

the generated relation is equality on E_n x E_m. In particular, for
E_2 x E_3 the witness records |R^wedge| = 32 and zero off-diagonal pairs.

The minimal binary generator count of the product cardinality is exactly

  ceil(log_2 |E_n x E_m|) = n + m

on power windows, witnessed for (n,m) = (1,1), (2,3), and (4,4). This is an
additive Kunneth-shaped shadow, but only at the level of log-cardinality.
It erases ordered bidegree: (1,4) and (2,3) both have dimension 5 while no
square bidegree has been constructed. The quantized dimension is still
base-dependent: 128 has dimensions 7 over base 2 and 5 over base 3, while
243 has dimensions 8 over base 2 and 5 over base 3.

Therefore the naive generated relation plus additive dimension does not
construct a CC square carrier, square H^2, intersection form,
correspondence algebra, Frobenius graph, Laurent boundary data, torsor
splitter, DD.4 weight>=1 datum, or positive Rosati involution.
```
