This candidate is stricter than active extension-depth in general:

```text

  the active layer generator is Delta_{p,q}, not an unnamed p/q
  interaction field.

layer creation:
  Fil_C^{Delta} is built from iterated Delta_{p,q} extension/cone
  operations, not from a pre-existing filtered complement.

  rho_G^{Delta} can vary while D, marking, scalar PF, complement

  passive readouts are held fixed.
```

## 6. Sharpened Theorem Target

The next deciding theorem is:

```text
PFV2-GPF-RMON-EXTCOMM-SELECT.
```

The theorem:

```text
There is a category or subcategory ECar_L1^{GPF,pq,ecomm} with objects
S_{p,q}^{G,ecomm}, a commutator-extension operator Delta_{p,q}, an
extension package E_C^{Delta}, an active filtration Fil_C^{Delta}, and
a filtered residue rho_G^{Delta} such that:

1. Delta_{p,q} is defined before all-places assembly from the p and q
   tower actions inside the p,q+complement GPF source;
2. iterated Delta_{p,q} extension/cone operations generate
   Fil_C^{Delta} while preserving GPF_{p,q} and Eff_{p,q};
3. rho_G^{Delta} is not recovered from D plus p,q marking;
4. rho_G^{Delta} is not scalar PF bookkeeping, complement
   bookkeeping, passive filtered shadow, associated graded data,
   presentation coherence, or gauge data;
5. kappa_G^{Delta} consumes rho_G^{Delta} before assembly;
6. changing rho_G^{Delta} while holding D, p,q marking, scalar PF,
   complement bookkeeping, presentation data, and passive filtered
   shadows fixed changes kappa_G^{Delta} or its target;
7. Epstein/DH controls cannot instantiate Delta_{p,q},
   Fil_C^{Delta}, rho_G^{Delta}, or kappa_G^{Delta} while retaining
   theta/count shadows;
8. B0_p and B0_q embed conservatively before rho_G^{Delta} is
   consumed;
9. rho_G^{Delta} remains load-bearing downstream in M_C/F_C/Tr_L.
```

This theorem is narrower than PFV2-GPF-RMON-EXTDEPTH-ACTIVE because it
commits active layer creation to a named commutator-extension operator.
