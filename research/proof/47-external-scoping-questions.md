required clauses A-E of doc 45 is impossible in every relevant

prove RH; it would produce a candidate-native liquid/index object on

## 6. Standing Rejection Rules

A proposed YES answer should be rejected, or at least demoted to
shadow data, if it has any of the following forms:

```text
1. It factors through Shv(R/(log p)Z; constant C) or any bare
   homotopy-type invariant of S^1.
2. It uses the Gaussian or continuum unit as an input normalization
   rather than deriving it inside the candidate category.
3. Its odd eigenvalues are the acting map's own degree, scale, or
   chosen correspondence parameter.
4. Its two evaluations are connected by transport, Poisson summation,
   coker=det algebra, or regularization of one series.
5. Its positivity is certified only on a strict family, scanned
   finite window, or hand-picked face.
6. It computes verbatim for DH or Epstein.
7. It reads zero data, explicit-formula signs, or RH-equivalent
   input before constructing the carrier.
```

Passing these rejection rules is not success. It only means the answer
has reached the level where the doc 45 package can be tested.
