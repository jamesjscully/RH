## 1. Purpose

Doc 240 says category labels, Tate twists, and realizations are downstream
of a square. The next repair is to change the ambient geometry:

```text

use pro-etale descent, completion, analytic stacks, and completed tensoring;

```

This is still not construction of `End(S^2/Z)`. Condensed and liquid
analytic structures can carry topological and analytic enrichment, but the
square needs a carrier-native object over `Z`, proper correspondences, two
degree maps, fiber-product composition, a Frobenius graph, projection

manufacture those arithmetic correspondences.

methods downstream of a future zeta-native square whose carrier and
