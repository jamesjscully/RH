## 1. Purpose

After doc 332, the cheap repair is:

```text

then route the remaining keys 3 and 6 through factorization, boundary-null
bookkeeping, or later endpoint/count/control filters.
```

That is not an internal image. It preserves the 2-power product
`z*(L*F22)` but deletes the whole mixed product `z*C3`. A square that makes
endpoint integrality, DD.4 boundary, graph/composition, Rosati, count, and
control death together must construct the mixed-support internalization
before those downstream facts are invoked.

The live branch therefore narrows from "one internal image" to:

```text

same internal Qbar_BECD effectivity domain, retaining the product
coefficients and signed support of 3 and 6 while also retaining the native
2-power subtowers.
```
