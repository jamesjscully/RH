separate the displayed control, but only after the control has already become
a graph object. That violates the formation-death requirement.

The live branch therefore narrows from "graph-formation death gate" to:

```text

count, and control death in one carrier-side formation path.
```

Controls, DH, and Epstein must fail by not being objects of this source fiber,
not by failing a later graph predicate.
