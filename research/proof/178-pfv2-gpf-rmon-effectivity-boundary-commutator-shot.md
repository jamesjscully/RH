## 3. Why This Is A Real Shot

This candidate is stricter than native extcomm in general:

```text
native supplier:
  Delta_{p,q}^{Eff} is supplied by Eff_{p,q}, not by an ambient exact
  or stable closure.

effectivity boundary:
  the obstruction measures non-interchange of p/q effectivity lifts,
  not a formal difference of endomorphisms.

source closure:
  E_C^{EffDelta} lives in the effectivity fiber of the
  p,q+complement GPF source.

passive-shadow independence:
  rho_G^{EffDelta} must vary while D, scalar PF, p,q marking,
  complement bookkeeping, presentation data, passive filtered shadows,
  and ambient cone shadows are fixed.

not first-map inert:
  kappa_G^{EffDelta} must change if rho_G^{EffDelta} changes while
  all readout shadows are fixed.
```

Thus the shot gives native commutator-extension a concrete supplier:
the effectivity boundary.

## 6. Sharpened Theorem Target

The next deciding theorem is:

```text
PFV2-GPF-RMON-EFFCOMM-SELECT.
```

The theorem:

```text
There is a category or subcategory ECar_L1^{GPF,pq,effcomm} with
objects S_{p,q}^{G,effcomm}, a native effectivity boundary
partial_Eff, an effectivity-boundary commutator Delta_{p,q}^{Eff}, an
effectivity-fiber extension package E_C^{EffDelta}, an active
filtration Fil_C^{EffDelta}, and a filtered residue
rho_G^{EffDelta} such that:

1. partial_Eff and Delta_{p,q}^{Eff} are defined before all-places
   assembly inside Eff_{p,q};
2. Delta_{p,q}^{Eff} measures non-interchange of p/q effectivity lifts,
   not a formal ambient commutator;
3. iterated Delta_{p,q}^{Eff} extensions generate Fil_C^{EffDelta}
   inside the effectivity fiber while preserving GPF and effectivity;
4. rho_G^{EffDelta} is not recovered from D plus p,q marking;
5. rho_G^{EffDelta} is not scalar PF bookkeeping, complement
   bookkeeping, passive filtered shadow, ambient cone data,
   associated graded data, presentation coherence, or gauge data;
6. kappa_G^{EffDelta} consumes rho_G^{EffDelta} before assembly;
7. changing rho_G^{EffDelta} while all readout shadows are fixed
   changes kappa_G^{EffDelta} or its target;
8. Epstein/DH controls cannot instantiate partial_Eff,
   Delta_{p,q}^{Eff}, rho_G^{EffDelta}, or kappa_G^{EffDelta} while
   retaining theta/count shadows;
9. B0_p and B0_q embed conservatively before rho_G^{EffDelta} is
   consumed;
10. rho_G^{EffDelta} remains load-bearing downstream in M_C/F_C/Tr_L.
```

This theorem is narrower than PFV2-GPF-RMON-EXTCOMM-NATIVE because it
commits the native operator to the effectivity boundary.
