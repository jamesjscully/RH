# 45 - Final missing-structure specification

Status: 2026-06-11. Consolidates docs 41-44 with the liquid-slot
requirements of MM. This is the current final characterization of the
missing structure. It is not a construction, and it is not a proof of
RH.

## 1. One-Sentence Target

Find a liquid/metrized Euler-product carrier with a candidate-native
continuum unit such that a forced-nonnegative Frobenius-index/Gram
comparison exhausts the full Sonin/Frobenius-graph cone and is
independently evaluated by a nonempty odd spectral slot whose
eigenvalues are supplied by the carrier, not by the acting
correspondence.

Everything below expands this sentence into checkable obligations.

An arriving candidate must be presented as:

```text

```

```text

  A liquid/gaseous/metrized symmetric monoidal setting, not a bare
  sheaf category. It must be rich enough to remember log p as metric or
  analytic-ring data.

b
  A conservative embedding of the banked arithmetic carriers into L:
  the THH/TP/TR towers, the strict count layer, the ku face, and the
  Frobenius graph/index faces. Banked identities may not be rescaled or
  destroyed.

R/T
  Internal R and T = R/Z objects, with the missing archimedean corner
  present. Bare torsion shadows are insufficient.

U
  The candidate-native continuum unit. It must be derived inside L,
  not inserted. Its Mellin/Fourier data may control slot constants, but
  object conditions must survive unit covariance.

Fr
  A class-and-scale Frobenius action/correspondence that queries the
  carrier. It must not supply the odd-slot eigenvalues by definition.

H_odd
  A nonempty odd spectral/determinant/cohomological slot. The slot's
  eigenvalues must be unforced: neither empty as at Spec F_q nor
  self-supplied as on the solenoid.

I
  A forced-nonnegative index/Gram/count form. The sign must come from
  what the values are, such as dimensions, counts, Gram norms, or
  index-level integers.

N
  The class family on which I is evaluated. It must exhaust the full
  doc-00 Sonin core / HH Frobenius-graph cone, including k=1 boundary
  directions, deep graph steps, mixed towers, and real/conic directions.

Count
  An independent count-side evaluation with a natural domain larger
  than the agreement locus.

Compare
  A theorem equating the index/Gram and count evaluations on the common
  locus, with P3-grade independence: distinct structures, contentful
  agreement, and a nontrivial unforced spectrum.
```

## 3. Required Clauses

### A. Liquid Address

```text

  log p must live in metrized/liquid/analytic-ring/equivariant/kernel
  data. Bare Shv(R/(log p)Z; C), ordinary localizing invariants of
  circles, and plain solenoid entropy are not enough.

A2 internal R
  L must contain internal R and T=R/Z with the archimedean completion
  absent from the banked finite-adelic Anderson layer.

A3 Fourier self-duality
  The Gaussian/Fourier fixed unit must be structural, not chosen.
  More generally, the candidate's own unit controls slot data by its
  own Mellin/Fourier laws.

A4 trace coupling
  The trace must couple comb and continuum on the same index/Gram form.
  A separate positive archimedean block is the wrong shape.
```

### B. Euler-Product Carrier

```text
B1 prime-power address
  Prime-power classes and graph depths must be structural.

B2 carrier guard
  DH and Epstein must fail by non-existence/undefinability of the
  carrier or coupling, while their theta/PD shadows may remain alive.

B3 no carrier overreach
  A construction that computes verbatim for Epstein or DH has not found
  the load-bearing Euler-product carrier.
```

### C. Unit And Threading

### D. Index Positivity And Exhaustion

```text
D1 forced nonnegativity
  The second evaluation must be forced nonnegative by its native
  structure: dimension, count, Gram norm, or index-level integrality.

D2 full graph-cone exhaustion
  Positivity must range over the whole Sonin/Frobenius-graph core, not
  a strict family, scanned window, or hand-picked face.

D3 candidate-native unit
  The graph-cone test must use the candidate's own unit and covariant
  slot data. Hand-inserted strict-face repairs do not certify BC13.

D4 uniformity
  The class/scale family must persist along the infinite support
  filtration, not only in a finite numerical panel.
```

### E. Second Evaluation

```text
E1 P3 independence
  The count and spectral/index evaluations must have different natural
  extension domains. A chain of natural isomorphisms, Poisson transport,
  coker=det algebra, or regularization of one series is one evaluation.

E2 nonempty odd slot
  The odd determinant/spectral slot must be nonzero.

E3 unforced eigenvalues
  The odd-slot eigenvalues must be supplied by the carrier/slot
  structure independently of the acting map. The acting correspondence
  queries them; it does not define them.

E4 contentful theorem
  The agreement between evaluations must consume mathematics beyond the
  definitions of the two sides.
```

## 4. Negative Boundary

The following routes are now classified and should not be re-promoted
without genuinely new structure.

```text

  Scale-blind by Efimov-style localizing-invariant results; cannot
  produce log p or kappa.

solenoid S_p
  Has address and nonempty H^1, but the eigenvalue is self-supplied by
  multiplication-by-p.

  Gives the right two-evaluation pattern, but the point has empty odd
  spectrum and forced Adams eigenvalues.

p-complete TP/TR/Witt
  Transport or p-adic blindness unless an external iota/archimedean
  move is supplied.

strict square / hand-inserted unit
  Posable and useful, but fails Frobenius-graph exhaustion.

ku face
  Best theorem-grade supply carrier and depth-wall model, but one
  evaluation only.

max-Gram / signature flow
  Certifies Lorentzian shape, but is supply-grade or carrier-blind;
  it does not supply the second evaluation.

Gaussian silhouette / Delta_p
  Unit-slot data. Shape and payment laws are covariant with the
  candidate's own unit.

Gaussian deep-cell negativity
  Slot-phase data; self-dual Hermite already flips the banked cell.
```

No banked carrier is a decision-grade survivor.
