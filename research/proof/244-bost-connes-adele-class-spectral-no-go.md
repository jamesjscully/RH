## 1. Purpose

Earlier cycles killed the related but weaker repairs: archimedean family
residue-field suppliers, F1 constant towers, regularized determinants,
type-II_1 trace, adelic restricted products, automorphic trace-formula
packets, and Arakelov intersection packages. The noncommutative-geometric
repair is tempting because it has exactly the vocabulary one wants:

```text
time evolution, KMS states, partition function zeta(beta),
adele-class space, scaling action, spectral realization, trace formula.
```

Those are real zeta-organizing structures. They are not, by themselves,
construction of `End(S^2/Z)`. The target needs a zeta-native square over
`Z`, `rep_NS`, two degree maps, proper correspondence category,
fiber-product composition, carrier-native Frobenius graph, projection
formula, and positive Rosati. BC/KMS/spectral data gives a dynamical and
trace-theoretic package; it does not manufacture the square correspondence
calculus.

This document rejects only the shortcut:

```text

correspondence carrier required by End(S^2/Z).
```

It does not reject BC/KMS, the adele-class space, spectral triples, or trace
formulae downstream of a future global square whose degree maps,
correspondences, projection formula, Frobenius graph, and Rosati are already
carrier-native.
