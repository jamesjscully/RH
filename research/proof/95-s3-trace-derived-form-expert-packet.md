## 1. Minimal Context

We are trying to decide whether the S3 kernel slot can become a B1
proposal before any numerical witness.

The required shape is:

```text
K_B1(C) = Tr_L(b1(comb_C) acting through one mixed object M_C)
```

where:

```text
1. b1(comb_C) and R_L/T_C both enter M_C before evaluation;
2. the output lands in one same-form codomain F_C;
3. F_C is an index, Gram, count, or stronger candidate-native object;
4. the valuation/depth form on F_C is derived by the trace theorem,
   not chosen as a weight, window, bilinear form, stress parameter,
   determinant wrapper, or regularization;
5. Epstein/DH controls are unformable at carrier/effectivity while
   theta/count shadows may remain meaningful.
6. the trace domain is strict enough that Epstein/DH controls are not
   objects at the first carrier-consuming map.
7. the first carrier-consuming map occurs before M_C, F_C, Tr_L, or any
   tensor/sum/completion/pushforward/coend/kernel/correspondence/
   determinant/cokernel/trace-class closure that is already available
   for monoid, measure, coefficient, or represented-norm shadows.
```

Docs 74, 93, and 100-102 already reject:

```text
generic theta/count/Poisson/discrete-measure kernels;
valuation/depth decoration read by externally supplied forms.
mixed objects, codomains, or traceable objects formed by broad
shadow-stable operations before primitive carrier checking.
```

The question is whether a trace theorem can derive the form and
codomain natively.
