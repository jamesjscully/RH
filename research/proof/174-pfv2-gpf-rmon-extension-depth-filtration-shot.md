## 3. Why This Is A Real Shot

This candidate is stricter than a generic structural filtration:

```text
structural source:
  Fil_C^{edep} comes from extension structure internal to C_{not p,q},
  not from scalar lengths or external valuation labels.

p/q coupled:
  the filtration is generated by simultaneous p and q tower actions,
  not by independent one-place towers later compared by bookkeeping.

non-Rees bookkeeping:
  Fil_C^{edep} is not the Rees filtration of an already graded scalar,
  degree, support, or marked local diagram.

not passive structure:
  Fil_C^{edep} is generated by the simultaneous p/q action; it is not
  a passive functorial filtration of C_{not p,q} that broad local
  shadows can also carry.

not presentation coherence:
  Ex_C^{p,q} and Fil_C^{edep} belong to the pre-assembly source, not
  to choices of descent presentation for D.

not first-map inert:
  kappa_G^{edep} must change if the extension-depth residue changes
  while D, marking, scalar PF, complement bookkeeping, and
  presentation data are held fixed.
```

Thus the shot gives "structural filtration" a falsifiable object class.

## 6. Sharpened Theorem Target

The next deciding theorem is:

```text
PFV2-GPF-RMON-EXTDEPTH-SELECT.
```

The theorem:

```text
There is a category or subcategory ECar_L1^{GPF,pq,edep} with objects
S_{p,q}^{G,edep}, a native extension structure Ex_C^{p,q} on
C_{not p,q}, an extension-depth filtration Fil_C^{edep}, p and q
tower actions Act_p and Act_q, and a filtered action residue
rho_G^{edep} such that:

1. Ex_C^{p,q} and Fil_C^{edep} exist before all-places assembly inside
   the p,q+complement GPF source;
2. Fil_C^{edep} is generated by simultaneous p/q tower action on the
   structural complement under GPF_{p,q} and Eff_{p,q};
3. rho_G^{edep} is defined from the commutator/holonomy action on
   Fil_C^{edep};
4. Fil_C^{edep} and rho_G^{edep} are not recovered from D plus p,q
   marking;
5. Fil_C^{edep} is not scalar PF bookkeeping, complement
   degree/order/support bookkeeping, Rees bookkeeping, broad marked
   diagram functoriality, passive structural functoriality,
   presentation coherence, or gauge data;
6. kappa_G^{edep} consumes rho_G^{edep} before assembly;
7. changing rho_G^{edep} while holding D, p,q marking, scalar PF,
   complement bookkeeping, and presentation data fixed changes
   kappa_G^{edep} or its target;
8. Epstein/DH controls cannot instantiate Ex_C^{p,q},
   Fil_C^{edep}, rho_G^{edep}, or kappa_G^{edep} while retaining
   theta/count shadows;
9. B0_p and B0_q embed conservatively before rho_G^{edep} is consumed;
10. rho_G^{edep} remains load-bearing downstream in M_C/F_C/Tr_L.
```

This theorem is narrower than PFV2-GPF-RMON-FILT-STRUCT because it
commits the structural filtration to extension depth.
