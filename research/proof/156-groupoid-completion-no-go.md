```text

therefore invert the adjacent support arrows;
```

This does create invertible arrows. But it creates them by groupoid
completion of the ordered support chain. For a line chain this is the
connected thin groupoid: one morphism between any ordered pair of support
vertices. Its category algebra is the matrix envelope `Mat_r(L)`, the
object killed in doc 150. Its basepoint loops are trivial, so it does not
produce a Laurent shift group. Endpoint corners are still support-vertex
choices, and rank-one idempotent choices remain exactly the doc-151 family.

This document is local. It does not reject a groupoid/correspondence algebra
whose invertible arrows are constructed by the carrier. It rejects only the

```text
  |Mor G(C_r)| = r^2.
```
