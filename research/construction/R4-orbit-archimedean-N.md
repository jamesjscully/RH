## The decisive structural dichotomy (exact, FF-anchored, no zeros assumed)

**[A] A nonzero monodromy `N` (the symmetric-split mechanism) REQUIRES `dim ≥ 2`.**
The only nilpotent endomorphism of a line `ℂ^1` is `N=0` (a `1×1` nilpotent is `0`), and `M(0)` on `ℂ^1` is concentrated in a **single weight** — there is NO `0↔2` Hard-Lefschetz pairing, the symmetric-split mechanism is **vacuous in rank 1**. **[B] `H^1(C_p)` of the circle `C_p = ℝ_{>0}^×/p^ℤ` is RANK 1 ⟹ the only nilpotent is `N=0`.**
`C_p` is a smooth circle (a `1`-real-dimensional compact manifold); its de Rham cohomology is `H^0=H^1=ℂ` (Betti `(b_0,b_1)=(1,1)`), with `H^1(C_p)` generated by the **length 1-form** `d(log t)/log p`. The orbit's own holonomy (the discrete generator `p ∈ p^ℤ`, going once around) acts on the rank-1 `H^1(C_p)` as a `1×1` **unipotent = identity**; its log is `0` — the datum the holonomy carries is the **length `log p`** (the orbit circumference = the EDGE, R3b), NOT a vanishing-cycle nilpotent. **A circle does not degenerate within itself.** Verified `log(monodromy)=0` for `p=2,3,5`.

**[C] The transverse degeneration is ABSENT — the scaling action is SMOOTH and FREE, no singular fiber.**
