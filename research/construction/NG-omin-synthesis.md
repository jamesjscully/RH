## 1. Is the BKT properization mechanism real? — YES (a living theorem, right kind of work)

Confirmed against primary sources (arXiv:1811.12230 Invent. math. 2022; Peterzil–Starchenko Duke 2013;
Bakker–Klingler–Tsimerman period-map definability):

- **Definable Chow** (Peterzil–Starchenko): a definable + analytic subset of a complex algebraic variety
  is **algebraic**. Engine = a Liouville/o-minimal finiteness theorem (entire definable holomorphic
  functions are exactly polynomials; a definable real-analytic function ≢0 has finitely many zeros).
- **o-minimal GAGA** (Bakker–Brunebarbe–Tsimerman): `Coh(X) → Coh(X^def)` is fully faithful and exact on
  a separated `S/ℂ`; with **Artin algebraization** the load-bearing corollary is
  **"PROPER definable images of complex algebraic spaces are algebraic."**
- **Period maps** are definable in `R_an,exp` (Siegel-set bounded fundamental domain + `exp` for
  nilpotent-orbit boundary asymptotics), hence o-minimal GAGA forces them **algebraic**.

So o-minimality genuinely converts an archimedean *transcendental* object (a period integral, no algebraic
formula) into an *algebraic/finite* one. The mechanism is real and does the right **kind** of work
(infinite/transcendental → finite/algebraic). **MECHANISM: REAL.**

**But the decisive structural fact (all three angles isolate it independently):** the conversion fires
**only for objects that are (i) DEFINABLE and (ii) PROPER, and PROPERNESS is an INPUT, not an output.**
O-minimality supplies *tame finiteness* (cell decomposition, Definable-Chow/Liouville finiteness); Artin
supplies *proper → projective*. The o-minimal structure does **not manufacture** the proper boundary — it
algebraizes an *already-proper* definable image. Properness/finite-type-over-`ℂ` of the source is the
o-minimal analogue of the imported finite ground.

**ARCHIMEDEAN FIBRE: WILD.** And it is wild **exactly where RH lives** — the infinitely many critical
